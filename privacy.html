Privacy Policy - Heny Property Agent

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This app is a private internal tool used solely by its owner to publish real estate listing content (photos and captions) to their own social media accounts (Threads, Instagram, and TikTok).

What data this app handles

The app processes property listing photos and captions supplied directly by the account owner. It does not collect, request, or process personal data from any third party, and it does not read, store, or share data belonging to other TikTok, Instagram, or Threads users.

What this app does not do

  • It does not collect data from end users interacting with the published posts.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Data retention

Listing photos and captions are retained only as needed to publish and archive the account owner's own real estate listings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact details listed on the associated social media profiles.
